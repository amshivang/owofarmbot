HOW TO USE GETTING STARTED GUIDE
STEP 01

Extract Discord Token

Log in to Discord via your browser, open DevTools (F12), inspect any network request, and copy the Authorization header token value.

STEP 02

Verify Account Connection

Click "Start Farming" to open the dashboard, paste your token, and verify the connection. This unlocks all farming settings and fetches your profile.

STEP 03

Configure Settings

Enable or disable gambling, set bet amounts, choose pray/curse command targets and limits. Configurations are dynamically saved.

STEP 04

Launch Farm Bot

Press "Start Farmbot" and monitor live terminal logs. The console automatically scrolls and prunes entries to prevent memory lag.
